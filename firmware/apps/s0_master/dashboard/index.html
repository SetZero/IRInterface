Unlock meter

Meters without a button are operated by flashing light at their optical port. The read head can do that with its LED: a short flash steps through the display, a long one changes a setting, and the PIN is entered as one flash per count of each digit. The PIN comes from your metering point operator; it is used once and not stored here. The meter's manual has the exact procedure.
